Seller Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: July 2026 • Platform Agreement for Merchant Partners

1 You Set Your Own Price
The price you enter is the exact amount you receive. If you list a product for ₹1,000, you receive ₹1,000 (subject to the return policy rules below).
2 No Commission for Your First 30 Days*
For your first 30 days after onboarding, Hive charges 0% commission on all products listed with returns enabled.
3 If You Disable Returns
If you choose not to offer customer returns, a flat 2% platform fee will be deducted from the price you set for every order.
4 Wrong Items Must Be Accepted Back
If the wrong item, incorrect size, or defective product is dispatched, the customer will be refunded immediately and the item will be returned to your store. Wrong-item returns are mandatory and non-negotiable.
5 Repeated Wrong Orders
Sending incorrect products 3 times within a single week will automatically flag your merchant account. If this continues across 3 separate weeks, your store may be permanently removed from Hive. Any pending eligible payments will still be settled to your bank account.
6 Choose Your Return Policy
You may select one of two store-wide policies:
  • Enable returns for all products (0% commission during promo)
  • Disable returns for all products (2% platform fee applies)
Note: This policy applies to your entire store inventory, not individual products.
7 Settlement & Payment Schedule
  • Returns enabled: Payment is released automatically after the customer return/exchange window closes.
  • Returns disabled: Payment is released according to the standard weekly settlement schedule.
8 Keep Your Stock Updated
You agree to maintain accurate stock levels:
  • Before closing your boutique each business day.
  • Immediately whenever an item is sold offline in your physical store.
9 Sell Only Available Products
Only list items that are physically in stock and ready for dispatch. If an item becomes unavailable, mark it out of stock immediately.
10 Seller Agreement Binding
By confirming this agreement, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by all Hive Merchant Partner Terms & Conditions.
